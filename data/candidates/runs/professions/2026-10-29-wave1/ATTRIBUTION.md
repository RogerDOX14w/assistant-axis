# Attribution

Sources of run professions/2026-10-29-wave1, generator B (lists of professions; coding plan 16, section 7d).

This work uses information from the O*NET 31.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license. O*NET(R) is a trademark of USDOL/ETA.

Changes: the lay role names and one-sentence descriptions in this run were derived from O*NET occupation titles, descriptions and job titles by a language model (Claude Haiku 5.5), and the occupations were grouped by their 2018 SOC broad codes.  USDOL/ETA has not approved, endorsed, or tested these modifications.

Employment figures: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), May 2025 national estimates, https://www.bls.gov/oes/ (public domain; cited as BLS asks).
