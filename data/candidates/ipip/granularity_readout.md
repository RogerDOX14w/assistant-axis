# IPIP clusters: the granularity readout

Two signals, read together and against each other, never against an absolute bar (QUESTIONS 50, Roger 2026-10-11).  **Signal 1, too small**: each pole read against the nearest pole of *another* cluster, by name and gloss, with M3's pinned overlap call (rubric A, one pair per call, Sonnet 5.5); a reading of **3** ("the same concept, differing only in scope, degree or emphasis") rather than 2 often means one trait was split across two clusters.  **Signal 2, too big**: the clusters printed in full below, chosen to span the size range, for reading.  Both are reported by cluster-size band, because one threshold can over-split a dense region and under-split a sparse one at the same time; on a tie the **finer** cut is preferred, since a too-small pair is recoverable at the end-of-waves grouping while a too-big cluster hides the traits inside it.

The within-pole item pairs are the third, cheaper view (each item shown as a trait, "I <statement>"); single items are narrower than traits, so those absolute levels may sit low.  The nearest-**corpus**-trait column of each cluster row is a different thing and is not a size signal: it is novelty.
