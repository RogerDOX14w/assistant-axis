The check's own verdicts are not shown, so your marks stay independent of them; the generator of each row is in the side-car ([sheet_key.json](./sheet_key.json)), not here.

**Marks** (second column): **Y** fits; **S** secondary sense (the label can mean it, but said of a person it usually means something else); **N** not a sense in ordinary English; **?** unsure.  A word after the mark is welcome (a better label, or why).  Leave a row blank to skip it.
