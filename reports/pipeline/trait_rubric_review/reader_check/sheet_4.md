>     - May not be effective in time-sensitive situations.
>   - **Best for**: Creative teams, knowledge-based industries, and organizations valuing employee development.
>
> ---
>
> ### 3. **Laissez-Faire Leadership**
> - **Description**: Minimal guidance is provided; employees are given freedom to make decisions.
> - **Impact on Productivity**:
>   - **Pros**:
>     - Encourages independence and innovation.
>     - Suitable for highly skilled, self-motivated teams.
>   - **Cons**:
>     - Often leads to confusion, lack of direction, and inconsistent performance.
>     - Not suitable for most employees without strong self-discipline.
>   - **Best for**: Remote teams of experienced professionals, artists, or in R&D environments.
>
> ---
>
> ### 4. **Transformational Leadership**
> - **Description**: Focuses on inspiring and motivating employees to exceed their own expectations.
> - **Impact on Productivity**:
>   - **Pros**:
>     - High employee engagement and loyalty.
>     - Encourages innovation, personal growth, and organizational change.
>     - Strong long-term performance.
>   - **Cons**:
>     - Can be demanding on the leader.
>     - May not work well in rigid or highly regulated environments.
