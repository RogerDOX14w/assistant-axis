# Role rubric check: 40 pairs to mark

For the role versions of M3's rubrics ([overlap_concept_roles.md](./rubrics/overlap_concept_roles.md), [relation_roles.md](./rubrics/relation_roles.md)), before the pilot's first overlap call ([coding_plan_16_role_generators.md](./coding_plan_16_role_generators.md) § 8, the paid checks).  About twenty minutes.

**How to mark.**  Put one answer in the `mark` column of each row, on the overlap rubric's scale, reading the target against the other role: `4` the same role under another name; `3` the target is a kind of the other, or the other narrowed to a setting, specialty or rank (a cardiologist with a doctor); `2` overlapping roles, neither a kind of the other, which includes the other being a kind of the target; `1` neighbouring roles (same world, different work); `0` different roles; `opposite` the counterpart (the other side of the same relation); `unsure`.  The direction matters for `3` and `2`.

**What the columns are.**  `target` and `other` are corpus roles (their files linked); the two descriptions are what the check sends the model (the target's is its role gloss, written by the role gloss call, the other's the corpus file's).  `novelty_score.py rubric-check --pairs` reads this file's `mark` column by its header and the pair ids; the design of the pairs is in [role_rubric_pairs_key.json](./role_rubric_pairs_key.json) (not needed for marking).
