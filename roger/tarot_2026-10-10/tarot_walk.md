# The 22 Major Arcana as gap-filling inspiration (2026-10-10)

Written for Roger.  His decision (2026-10-10): the cards are not imported; each card, **read as describing a person**, is a prompt for the question "is this kind of person covered?", and a gap gets a plain trait, not under the card's name, as was done for Thomas-Kilmann and the occupational instruments.

**Method.**  Each card's person reading was written from Waite's own text ([The Pictorial Key to the Tarot](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot), 1911, public domain: the figures of Part II § 2, [waite_part2_trumps_major.json](./waite_part2_trumps_major.json), and the divinatory meanings of Part III § 3, [waite_part3_s3_greater_arcana_meanings.txt](./waite_part3_s3_greater_arcana_meanings.txt)); the readings, with the phrases each rests on, are in [tarot_person_readings.json](./tarot_person_readings.json).  Each reading was embedded the way the trait-gap novelty check embeds a candidate (text-embedding-3-large, centred; [nearest_traits.py](./nearest_traits.py), [nearest_traits.json](./nearest_traits.json)).  Where the shortlist was weak, a plain word was put through the trait-gap probe (filter, then the novelty check with its 0-4 overlap readings, the card's reading as the gloss hint; [probe/](./probe/), $0.20).  Cosines in the centred space below about 0.15 are close to noise.

**Result: 17 cards covered, four gaps covering five cards.**  The four drafts and their review: [drafts.json](./drafts.json), [review.md](./review.md) (being written).
